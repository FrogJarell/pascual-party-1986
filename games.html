Timer Challenge

Two players. One goal time. No looking!

How it works:

  • Host sets a secret goal time (e.g., 4 seconds)
  • Each player has a buzzer — press when you THINK the time is up
  • You CANNOT see the timer!
  • Closest to the goal time wins the round
